# Avatar asset attribution

The modular heads, eyes, and hairstyles in this folder are adapted from the
[Universal LPC Spritesheet Character Generator](https://github.com/LiberatedPixelCup/Universal-LPC-Spritesheet-Character-Generator).
They are used under the licenses listed by the project: OGA-BY 3.0,
CC-BY-SA 3.0, GPL 3.0, and GPL 2.0 where applicable.

Selected asset contributors include bluecarrot16, Benjamin K. Smith
(BenCreating), Stephen Challener (Redshrike), ElizaWy, JaidynReiman,
Manuel Riecke (MrBeast), Joe White, and Nila122. The images here contain only
the front-facing idle frame and have been resized with nearest-neighbor
scaling for Chronos.
